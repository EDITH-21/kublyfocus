🔒 ON-DEVICE PRIVACY GUARANTEED

Personal Dashboard is Private

To protect your personal study habits and focus history, your Knolect Focus Center is hosted exclusively inside the Knolect Browser Extension on your local device. Private usage data is never exposed through a public website.

How to Access Your Personal Focus Center

  1. Open your browser where Knolect is installed.
  2. Click the Knolect extension icon in your browser toolbar.
  3. Click Open Dashboard ↗ to view your private metrics, allowlisted channels, and study timers.
