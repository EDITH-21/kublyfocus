About Knolect

Learning deserves your attention.

Knolect is a learning-focused productivity project designed to help people use YouTube more intentionally. It aims to reduce distracting recommendation loops and create a more focused environment for educational viewing.

CLIENT-SIDE ARCHITECTURE

Built as a lightweight Manifest V3 browser extension for Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera), Knolect operates entirely on your device. It does not track your watch history, harvest your personal credentials, or monetize your attention.

The Motivation

Less distraction.
More intention.

YouTube hosts some of the world's most valuable educational content: complete university lecture series, deep coding tutorials, mathematical walkthroughs, and scientific seminars.

Yet the platform's default interface is optimized to maximize watch time through autoplay, algorithmic sidebars, Shorts reels, and sensational recommendations. Learners routinely open YouTube for a single 20-minute lecture, only to be pulled into hours of unrelated video loops.

Knolect was created to restore learner agency. By establishing an intentional boundary between educational study and endless browsing, it helps you finish the lesson you set out to learn.

THE REALITY OF DIGITAL STUDY
The Algorithmic Pull: Default feeds pull learners toward high-arousal entertainment, breaking study flow and pulling attention away from core coursework.
The Knolect Standard: Study videos are protected. Entertainment rabbit holes are cleanly intercepted before they derail your session.
Product Principles

Our Approach to Focus

Four clear design decisions guide how Knolect protects your learning time.

01

Intentional learning over endless browsing

We invert the traditional blocker model. Instead of maintaining endless, fragile blocklists, Knolect lets you define your study objective and intercepts unapproved recommendations by default during deep sessions.

02

User control over personal study channels

You maintain final authority over your learning environment. You can explicitly allow study channels with one click, while our system handles channel ID resolution so your whitelist stays durable.

03

Focus tools that support deliberate sessions

Unbounded study sessions lead to fatigue and drift. Knolect integrates session timers—such as 25-minute Pomodoro cycles—to help pace your effort and create clear starting and ending boundaries.

04

Clear, transparent controls over blocking

You decide exactly what is filtered. Granular toggles allow you to silence comments, hide recommendation sidebars, remove Shorts, or clear the home feed entirely, without hidden algorithms.
