The Distraction Problem

The Recommendation Trap

YouTube holds the world's greatest educational lectures. Yet the default interface is engineered to pull learners away from coursework.

Default YouTube

The Algorithmic Pull

Every screen element is optimized for watch time, converting focused study sessions into fragmented attention.

  • ✕ Infinite Home Shelves: Sensational recommendations hijack your initial intention before you even begin studying.
  • ✕ Shorts & Rapid Feeds: Endless vertical reels fragment deep concentration and interrupt steady study pacing.
  • ✕ Related Sidebar Traps: Watch page sidebars suggest unrelated viral videos while you are trying to understand a lecture.
  • ✕ Search Traps: Typing a topic exposes you to clickbait thumbnails instead of structured curriculum.
With Knolect

The Allowlist Defense

Inverts the access model. Non-coursework recommendations are intercepted, leaving only intentional study.

  • ✓ Strict Focus Perimeter: Unapproved videos and distraction feeds are cleanly blocked by default during active focus.
  • ✓ Curated Study Allowlist: Verified educators (MIT OCW, 3Blue1Brown, freeCodeCamp) and your personal study channels play seamlessly.
  • ✓ Pomodoro Session Pacing: Built-in 15m, 25m, 45m, and 60m timers create clear boundaries and protect mental stamina.
  • ✓ Client-Side Interception: Operates locally inside your browser with zero browsing history or watch telemetry harvested.

Allowlist-First Blocking

Channel Allowlisting

Curate trusted creators (MIT OCW, 3Blue1Brown, freeCodeCamp) or save custom study channels directly from any active video. Allowlisted creators bypass blocking seamlessly.

Shorts & Feed Suppression

Eliminates high-stimulus Shorts shelves, navigation buttons, and infinite vertical reels. Prevents entertainment recommendations from dragging you into rabbit holes.

Pomodoro Session Timer

Timestamp-based focus timer (15m, 25m, 45m, 60m). Operates via background alarms, survives popup closing, and renders live countdown badges in your browser toolbar.

Local-First Storage

Zero browsing history harvesting, zero search logging, and no external tracking SDKs. All focus preferences and allowed channels reside safely in your browser local storage.

Granular Distraction Toggles

Selectively hide comments, suppress recommendation sidebars, remove Shorts, or mute thumbnails. Customise your learning environment exactly how you learn best.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Knolect.

What is Knolect? +

Knolect is a Manifest V3 Chromium extension that transforms YouTube into a focused learning environment by blocking recommendations, Shorts, comments, and home feeds while keeping educational videos usable.

Does it block YouTube Shorts? +

Yes. Knolect completely eliminates Shorts navigation links, sidebar buttons, feed shelves, and direct /shorts/* video URLs.

Can I search YouTube while Focus Mode is active? +

In Normal Focus Mode, search remains available. In Strict Focus Mode, search results are blocked with a clean block screen to prevent algorithmic distraction loops.

Can I allow my own study channels? +

Yes! You can add any channel by name, @handle, or YouTube URL in the popup. You can also click "+ Allow this channel" directly on any active video.

Does Knolect collect browsing history? +

No. Knolect does not collect, transmit, or store your YouTube watch history or search queries. All focus settings are stored locally in your browser sandbox.

Does it work after refreshing YouTube? +

Yes. All settings are persistently stored via chrome.storage.local. When you refresh or navigate inside YouTube, Knolect immediately re-applies focus rules.

Can I disable Focus Mode when I want? +

Yes. You can toggle Focus Mode off anytime from the popup or via the floating pill on the bottom-right of YouTube.

Which browsers are supported? +

Knolect is compatible with Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, and Opera via Developer Mode unpacked extension loading.
