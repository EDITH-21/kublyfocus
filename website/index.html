Core Capabilities

Built for Intentional Study

Every feature in Knolect is engineered to keep your attention on what matters.

🛡️

Strict Focus Mode

An allowlist-first security model. Blocks non-whitelisted videos, search rabbit holes, and direct entertainment URL entry during active sessions.

⭐

Channel Whitelist

Store your favorite educational creators (e.g. MIT OCW, 3Blue1Brown, freeCodeCamp). Whitelisted creators bypass blocking for seamless study.

🚫

Search & Shorts Blocking

Prevents entertainment searches and completely eliminates Shorts shelves, navigation buttons, and addictive vertical video feeds.

⏱️

Session Timer

Timestamp-based Pomodoro focus timer (15m, 25m, 45m, 60m). Runs via background alarms and survives popup closing with toolbar badge updates.

⚡

YouTube SPA Protection

Intercepts YouTube's single-page app transitions and dynamic navigation events so focus enforcement cannot be bypassed by clicking around.

🔒

100% Local Privacy

Zero video logging, zero search tracking, and no external history harvesting. All focus settings and whitelists remain securely on your device.
