Core Capabilities

Built for Serious Learners

Practical, real features implemented in the extension to create a calm YouTube workspace.

Focus Mode

Instantly hides algorithmic feeds, recommended videos sidebar, and distracting Shorts reels across YouTube.

Channel Whitelist

Exempt trusted educational creators (e.g., MIT OCW, 3Blue1Brown, freeCodeCamp) from strict blocking rules.

Session Timer

Pomodoro-style study session tracking (15m, 25m, 45m, 60m) with Chrome alarms and badge countdown updates.

Local Storage

All whitelist preferences and settings stay stored purely in your browser’s `chrome.storage.local` sandbox.

Distraction Controls

Toggle specific elements like comments, end screens, and home feed placeholders to match your workflow.

Privacy First

No login, no external telemetry server, and zero tracking scripts. Your viewing activity never leaves your device.
