The Distraction Problem

The Recommendation Trap

YouTube holds the world's greatest educational lectures. Yet the default interface is engineered to pull learners away from coursework.

Default YouTube

The Algorithmic Pull

Every screen element is optimized for watch time, converting focused study sessions into fragmented attention.

  • ✕ Infinite Home Shelves: Sensational recommendations hijack your initial intention before you even begin studying.
  • ✕ Shorts & Rapid Dopamine: Endless vertical reels disrupt deep concentration and break memory retention.
  • ✕ Related Sidebar Traps: Watch page sidebars suggest unrelated viral videos while you are trying to understand a lecture.
  • ✕ Search Traps: Typing a topic exposes you to clickbait thumbnails instead of structured curriculum.
With Knolect

The Allowlist Defense

Inverts the access model. Non-coursework recommendations are intercepted, leaving only intentional study.

  • ✓ Strict Focus Perimeter: Unapproved videos and distraction feeds are cleanly blocked by default during active focus.
  • ✓ Curated Study Allowlist: Verified educators (MIT OCW, 3Blue1Brown, freeCodeCamp) and your personal study channels play seamlessly.
  • ✓ Pomodoro Session Pacing: Built-in 15m, 25m, 45m, and 60m timers create clear boundaries and protect mental stamina.
  • ✓ Client-Side Interception: Operates locally inside your browser with zero browsing history or watch telemetry harvested.
