🚀 Quick Installation

Install BingeBlocker

Follow the simple steps below for your browser to activate distraction-free YouTube learning.

📦

Installation Instructions for Google Chrome

To run BingeBlocker locally or from source, you can load the unpacked extension directly via your browser's extensions manager in less than a minute.

1

Open Extension Management

Open a new tab in Google Chrome and paste the following address into your URL bar:

chrome://extensions
2

Enable "Developer mode"

Look in the top-right corner of the Extensions page and turn ON the toggle switch labeled Developer mode.

3

Load Unpacked Extension

Click the Load unpacked button that appears in the top-left toolbar, then select the root folder containing this extension's manifest.json.

4

Open YouTube & Pin the Extension

Navigate to youtube.com. Click the puzzle icon in your browser toolbar, pin BingeBlocker, and click it to toggle Focus Mode ON.

Verification Checklist

When Focus Mode is active on YouTube, your home feed is cleanly replaced by a focus hub, and sidebar recommendations on videos disappear.
