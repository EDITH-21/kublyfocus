Unpacked Setup

Install Knolect

Follow these quick steps to load the Knolect extension into your Chromium browser.

Step 1

Open Extension Management

Open a new tab in your browser and enter the extension management URL:

chrome://extensions/
Step 2

Enable Developer Mode

Locate the Developer mode toggle switch in the top-right corner of the extensions page and turn it ON.

Step 3

Download & Load Unpacked Extension

1. Download the knolect-extension.zip package.
2. Extract the ZIP archive into a folder on your computer.
3. Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top-left corner and select that extracted extension folder.

Step 4

Pin Knolect & Start Learning

Click the puzzle icon (🧩) in your browser toolbar and pin Knolect. Then visit YouTube to enjoy a distraction-free learning environment!
